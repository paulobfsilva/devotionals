# Week 1 — Making room for God each day

Status: outline for Paulo's review. Language: English. Scripture version: KJV. No publication dates assigned. This is an evergreen sample week, provisionally ordered Monday through Sunday; Sunday preparation is a proposal for review.

## Purpose and progression

Move from deliberately making private space for God into sincere worship, daily reading, bringing decisions before Him, listening, intercession and preparation for shared worship. Each day should stand on its own. The order describes an editorial sequence, not seven steps someone must complete to pray properly.

All sermon candidates below come from [Paulo's supplied sources](../../design/recovered-conversation.md). Their summaries are paraphrases, not quotations. Exact excerpts and their surrounding context still need source verification and Paulo's review before use. Scripture is given by reference at outline stage; full drafts will transcribe and check the KJV text.

## Monday — A place to meet your Father

**Scripture:** Matthew 6:5–8. Focus on private prayer and the Father's knowledge of our needs.

**Sermon candidate:** *Church Order*, 63-1226, paragraphs 84 and 92: private prayer and the distinction from leading public prayer.

**Editorial connection:** Make space for private communion within an ordinary day. Keep the sermon’s church-order setting clear when applying it to a personal routine.

**Prayer invitation:** What are you carrying today that others may not see? Bring it honestly before your Father.

**Guidance focus:** Help the person begin, then set the guide aside. If attention wanders, return to the thought of approaching the Father in private.

**Carry into the day:** Identify one workable place and opportunity to return tomorrow.

## Tuesday — Begin with worship

**Scripture:** Psalm 95:1–7. Focus on thanksgiving, worship and belonging to God.

**Sermon candidate:** *The Supernatural*, 56-0129, paragraph 34: sincere communion and worship before asking.

**Editorial connection:** Let what Scripture reveals about God shape the opening of prayer, while allowing honest requests to follow.

**Prayer invitation:** Which description of God in this passage draws your attention? Speak to Him about it, remembering a concrete reason for thanks.

**Guidance focus:** Offer room to remain with one thought of worship rather than collecting many things to say.

**Carry into the day:** Return to that reason for thanksgiving during an ordinary responsibility.

## Wednesday — Let the Word shape your response

**Scripture:** Psalm 119:33–40. Focus on asking for understanding and help to walk in God's ways.

**Sermon candidate:** *The Prophet Elisha*, 54-0723, paragraph 10: daily Bible reading and prayer.

**Editorial connection:** The passage itself contains petitions that can help the reader respond personally after reading. The sermon supports daily practice; it is not presented as an exposition of this Psalm.

**Prayer invitation:** Which request in these verses speaks to something you need today? Bring that need before God in your own words.

**Guidance focus:** Read slowly, notice a particular petition and connect it to a real part of life. Optional openings: understanding, willingness or attention.

**Carry into the day:** Keep one verse nearby to revisit when its concern arises.

## Thursday — Bring the decision before God

**Scripture:** Proverbs 3:5–7. Focus on trust, acknowledging God and the limits of one's own understanding.

**Sermon candidate:** *The Prophet Elisha*, 54-0723, paragraph 10: considering God before making decisions.

**Editorial connection:** Use a different emphasis from Wednesday despite drawing on the same paragraph. Ask Paulo whether this repetition strengthens continuity or calls for a different verified excerpt.

**Prayer invitation:** What decision are you trying to settle? Tell God what you hope for, what you do not understand, and where you need His direction.

**Guidance focus:** Allow honest desires and uncertainty, with time to ask for help to act according to His Word. Avoid promising an immediate answer or particular experience.

**Carry into the day:** Revisit the passage before taking the next step in that decision.

## Friday — Make room to listen

**Scripture:** Psalm 62:5–8. Focus on waiting upon God, trust and pouring out the heart before Him.

**Sermon candidate:** *Hear His Voice*, 58-1005M, paragraph 34: making room to listen after speaking in prayer.

**Editorial connection:** Hold honest expression and quiet waiting together. Quiet does not require manufacturing a message or a particular emotional state.

**Prayer invitation:** What do you need to pour out before God? After speaking, remain with a phrase from this passage and give room to listen.

**Guidance focus:** Open-ended quiet, with optional sound and guidance available on return. Consider significant thoughts in light of Scripture.

**Carry into the day:** Recall the passage when attention or worry scatters.

## Saturday — Make room for someone else

**Scripture:** 1 Timothy 2:1–6. Focus on intercession and the passage's grounding in Christ Jesus as mediator.

**Sermon candidate:** *What Visions Are*, 56-0421, paragraph 21: believers praying for one another in a worldwide prayer band.

**Editorial connection:** Personal prayer can include the needs of others. Preserve the original prayer-band context; do not turn its synchronized hours into a required schedule for this devotional.

**Prayer invitation:** Who comes to mind as you read? Bring their particular needs before God, including someone beyond your usual circle of concern.

**Guidance focus:** Help move from a name to thoughtful intercession without requiring a prayer list or personal data entry.

**Carry into the day:** Consider whether there is a practical act of care to offer.

## Sunday — Come prepared to worship

**Scripture:** Hebrews 10:19–25. Focus on drawing near, holding fast and encouraging one another in gathering.

**Sermon candidate:** *Jairus, A Secret Believer*, 55-0604, paragraph 6: prayer as a contribution to a meeting, including prayer for those in need.

**Editorial connection:** Private prayer can prepare someone to participate in congregational worship and care for others. The sermon addresses a particular meeting; its suggested hour or two is not a universal requirement for this session.

**Prayer invitation:** As you prepare to gather, who and what can you bring before God—the ministry, those attending, and your own readiness to receive and respond to His Word?

**Guidance focus:** Intercession for the service and a receptive heart. For someone unable to attend, leave room to pray for the congregation and seek an opportunity for encouragement.

**Carry into the day:** Look for one opportunity to encourage someone.

**For review:** Retain this Sunday emphasis, adapt it to the intended audience's service pattern, or make it an optional occasion-specific entry.
