# Devotionals — editorial template

## Agreed settings

- Initial theme: Making room for God each day.
- Language: English. Bible translation: King James Version (KJV).
- Initial theological and editorial reviewer: Paulo.
- Content must adhere to Biblical teaching and William Branham's teachings, including the established direction concerning prayer in Jesus' name. Specific interpretations require review rather than an assumption of approval.
- Scripture, sermon quotations and original commentary/guidance are separately identified.

## Weekly planning record

Record the theme, its Scriptural foundation, intended purpose, seven daily subjects and their relationship. Explain why the sequence is useful and how someone can join on any day. Record whether the week is evergreen or tied to an occasion; an occasion requires an audience/date decision.

Include proposed Scripture passages and sermon references before drafting commentary. Themes may be adjusted if the sources do not support the proposed connection. Plan the outline ahead of the full manuscripts.

## Daily entry template

### Editorial record

- Entry identifier, title, weekly theme and proposed position.
- Purpose: what this reading invites the person to consider and bring before God.
- Status: outline / draft / needs revision / approved / scheduled / published.
- Version, reviewer, review date and unresolved questions.

### Scripture

Record the passage reference and exact KJV text. Read surrounding verses to check context. Identify the particular detail that will shape reflection; keep sufficient context visible to the reader. Record the edition/source used for transcription checking.

### Supporting sermon excerpt

Record sermon title, date/code, paragraph number, exact excerpt and source location. Check against the full paragraph and surrounding context. Mark omissions transparently. Distinguish an excerpt supplied by Paulo from one independently checked against the source. Record publication-permission status before scheduling public release.

### Commentary, when useful

Explain the passage or its relationship to the excerpt in a short, concrete paragraph. Add only what helps understanding or meditation. Preserve each source's context; identify editorial connections as commentary rather than attributing them to the speaker. Length serves usefulness and is not yet fixed.

### Prayer invitation

Write one specific, open invitation connected to the reading. Help the person bring a situation, response or concern before God in their own words. Avoid prescribing what the person must feel or claiming knowledge of what God is telling them personally.

### Guided prayer continuation

For each movement that is useful, supply a brief invitation and enough reflection to help exploration. Use the first session's sequence as a starting framework: arrive, worship, respond, pray for someone, wait quietly, close. Do not require the person to answer every question or finish every movement.

The daily invitation supplies a starting point, not a limit on prayer. Let personal concerns enter naturally. Guidance remains available while the person prays; changes of movement are user-paced. Close with an invitation to pray in Jesus' name in the person's own words.

### Carry into the day

Offer one optional response connected to the reading. It may be something to remember, an act of care or a matter to bring before God again. No performance score or completion obligation.

## Paulo's review

- Does the interpretation adhere to Biblical teaching and William Branham's teachings?
- Are KJV text and sermon wording accurate, with sufficient context and traceable references?
- Does the selected sermon excerpt support the subject without forcing a connection?
- Are commentary and prompts distinguishable from source quotations?
- Does the invitation open into personal prayer, with enough depth for meditation?
- Is the guidance gentle, clear and free of prescribed duration or automatic progression?
- Does the day work independently while contributing something distinct to the week?
- Are theological questions and source/publication checks resolved for the version being approved?

Record approval of the specific version or requested revisions. AI may assist drafting and checking, but cannot mark Paulo's review complete. A changed interpretation or source excerpt returns to review.
